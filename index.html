// Running Test

RUNNING
TEST

Before we start training together, it helps to understand where you are right now. A short running test gives us a clear, objective baseline — so we can train smarter, set realistic goals and most importantly, keep you healthy and injury-free.

🛡️
Injury Prevention

Training hard is only effective if you stay healthy. The running test helps identify your current limits so we can build your load progressively and keep you away from overuse injuries.

🗣️
A Shared Language

Good coaching requires clear communication. The test establishes a mutual training language between us — so when I say "easy run" or "hard effort", we both know exactly what that means for your body.

🎯
Personalised Zones

Every athlete is different. The test allows me to define your personal training zones so every session — easy, moderate or hard — is calibrated precisely to your physiology.

📈
Track Your Progress

By repeating the test periodically throughout our coaching journey, we can objectively measure your improvement — not just feel it. Numbers don't lie.

Ready to find your baseline?

The test is simple, takes less than 30 minutes, and can be done anywhere. Get in touch and I'll guide you through everything.
